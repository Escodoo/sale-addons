Adds the concept of payment Escrow/Retention to blanket orders (Sale
Blanket Order) and to the invoices generated from them. Allows retaining
part of an invoice's amount (e.g. 20%) in an escrow account, reconciling
the invoice's open balance without it being treated as overdue/unpaid,
until the escrow is released.

Usage
=====

Prerequisite: a Receivable-type account must exist to be used as the
Escrow Account.

1. On the Blanket Order (Sale Blanket Order), fill in the **Escrow
   Account** and, optionally, the **Escrow Journal** (a journal of type
   "Miscellaneous").
2. Generate the Sale Order (SO) from the blanket order.
3. Create and confirm the invoice.
4. Register the normal payment for the non-retained part of the invoice
   (e.g. 80%).
5. On the invoice, click the **Escrow/Retention** button.
6. Fill in Date and Amount (and, if needed, Journal and Due Date) in the
   wizard and confirm.
7. A journal entry will be created (Credit: the invoice's receivable
   account / Debit: Escrow Account), automatically reconciled with the
   invoice. The invoice will display the **Payment Retained** status
   while the retention is not released.
8. To release the escrow at the end of the contractual cycle, manually
   reconcile the escrow account's debit line (e.g. with a release entry).
   The invoice will go back to the **Paid** status.
